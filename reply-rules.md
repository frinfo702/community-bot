# Reply rules

The bot may reply without a mention only when one of these applies.

Reply when:

- A member asks a question the bot can answer.
- A member asks for help, a lookup, a summary, or an opinion.
- The message continues a conversation the bot is already part of.
- A member refers to the bot by name without a mention.
- The bot has something specific to add that the conversation is missing.

Do not reply when:

- Members are talking to each other and the bot has nothing to add.
- The message is a greeting, a short reaction, or side chatter.
- The bot sent the last message and nobody has addressed it since.
- The message is a command for another bot or a system notice.
- The message is unclear and a reply would only be noise.
